Love Is War Drops 171.88 While Mikhawk Benches a 39.46-Point Maye

Love is War posts the week's high score in a 91.34-point demolition, and mikhawk loses by 4.58 with Drake Maye on the bench.

By the editor

Week 5 gave us a blowout, a heartbreaker, and a few upsets, and the blowout came first. Love is War (Miryth) hung 171.88 on Jared's Big Papa, who managed 80.54 for austinmorg. The 91.34-point margin was the week's biggest. Parker Washington put up 30.40, Malik Nabers added 29.10, and Jacoby Brissett chipped in 26.28 at quarterback. Brock Purdy led the Big Papa side with 17.16, which is not a number that wins anything.

That win lifts Love is War to 2-3 and eighth place, a record that looks wrong next to a league-best 690.40 points scored. The team is scoring like a contender and sitting like a middle-of-the-pack club. If the points keep coming, the wins should follow. Jared's Big Papa fell to 2-3 and 11th, and austinmorg could have used the 11.30 he left on the bench when Blake Corum (16.20) sat while Omarion Hampton (4.90) started. It wouldn't have saved him, but it would have been nice.

Now the tragedy. RomoCop (connal69) edged mikhawk 152.52 to 147.94, a 4.58-point nailbiter. Mikhawk had Drake Maye on the bench for 39.46 while Trevor Lawrence started and scored 22.74. That is a 16.72-point swing in a game decided by 4.58, and it is the Bench Blunder of the Week with the receipt attached. Mikhawk's 147.94 would have beaten 10 of the 13 other teams this week, and it still sits at 1-4. That's a Bad Beat of the Week for the ages, and a lesson in checking your lineup.

At the top, GopherDeeznuts (JorgusFantussy) is 4-1 and alone in first after a 147.20 to 87.80 win over THE crab warriors. Josh Allen scored 31.50, and the freshly added San Francisco 49ers defense chipped in 20.00. They Laported the Nabers (lakesleeper) have won three straight and climbed three spots to third. Bower Rangers, ranked 12th, knocked off second-ranked madmaxwellog49 in the week's biggest upset, and madmaxwellog49 has now lost two in a row.

Check Your Bench, Folks

Mikhawk, I have news that nobody wants to hear: Drake Maye scored 39.46 points on your bench. You started Trevor Lawrence (22.74) and lost by 4.58. Your 147.94 would have beaten 10 of the 13 other teams this week, and you're 1-4 anyway, 14th in the standings. It's the kind of loss that makes a manager stare at the wall. This column will sit with you in this moment, and then ask you to set your lineup earlier next time.

Elsewhere, a few reminders about what the waiver wire can do. Jared Goff, picked up for a bid of 150 in Week 2, has 68.78 points over 3 starts, and he gave madmaxwellog49 another 28.94 this week. It wasn't enough against Bower Rangers, but he's still our Waiver Wonder. Meanwhile, Love is War leads the league in points scored (690.40) and sits 2-3, which is the most Helmet Brigade thing I can think of. That kind of scoring doesn't stay unlucky forever, so expect Miryth to climb.

Finally, the trade desk. Love is War and Boomer Sooner swapped entire draft boards back in Week 1, rounds 1 through 14, and we'll be watching how that plays out. The Week 4 Ja'Marr Chase trade is tracked at 25.40 for They Laported the Nabers against 33.50 from DK Metcalf and Chris Olave for mikhawk, an 8.10 edge for mikhawk. Chase did his part this week with 19.70, and I'd love to know how the other side's receivers did. Mikhawk, check that bench first.
