Caleb Williams' 37.26 Powers madmaxwellog49 to Week 1's Top Score

Despite leaving a 33.80-point wideout on the bench, madmaxwellog49 posted 163.96 and opened the season as the league's lone 13-0 all-play team.

By the editor

Welcome to the 2026 season, Helmet Brigade, and what an opening statement from madmaxwellog49. Their 163.96 was the week's high score and a 38.20-point demolition of They Laported the Nabers. Quarterback Caleb Williams was the week's best player at 37.26, and Kenneth Walker (34.10) and Justin Jefferson (31.20) gave him plenty of help.

Here is the part that should frighten the other 13 managers. Jalen Coker sat on madmaxwellog49's bench and scored 33.80, while starter Chris Godwin managed 8.30. That is 25.50 points left on the pine, and the team still won by 38. The Gazette's all-play math has madmaxwellog49 beating all 13 other teams this week, and the playoff odds give them 97.4 percent to make the playoffs and 44.3 percent to take the top seed.

Chasing them are two teams that scored identical 138.36s. THE crab warriors, led by Ashton Jeanty's 32.70 and a 19.00 from kicker Evan McPherson, beat Jared's Big Papa by 11.24. GopherDeeznuts, with Josh Allen's 35.66 and Jaxon Smith-Njigba's 26.20, beat Bower Rangers by 11.26. Two points of margin separated by two hundredths, which is about as spooky as a season opener gets. GopherDeeznuts also posted a perfect 100.0 percent lineup efficiency, the only team to do so.

The Gazette also logged a week of benching crimes. Bower Rangers left Bryce Young's 32.44 on the bench for Matthew Stafford's 5.10 and lost by 11.26. Love is War lost by 2.32 with Caleb Douglas (14.40) sitting while Davante Adams scored 5.60. Both are our first lineup flips of the year, and it's only Week 1.
